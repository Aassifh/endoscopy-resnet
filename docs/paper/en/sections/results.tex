\Cref{tab:main} summarizes the main comparison on the Simula test split.
ImageNet transfer (C1) achieves the best macro-F1 (0.989) and polyp recall (0.971).
Domain CNN-JEPA with mask-aware SE (C4) reaches macro-F1 0.910 and polyp recall 0.829.
Scratch training (C0) is slightly below C4 (macro-F1 0.899).
ResNet-50 + CNN-JEPA (C2) reaches 0.913 macro-F1 but lower polyp recall (0.778).

\textbf{Ablations.} C3, C4, and C5 yield identical macro-F1 (0.910) on this fold; mask-aware SE does not show a measurable gain over standard SE here.

\textbf{Label efficiency (\Cref{tab:labels}, \Cref{fig:labelcurve}).}
C4 macro-F1 increases from 0.825 (10\% labels) to 0.907 (50\%) and 0.910 (100\%), but remains below C1 at all observed fractions.

\textbf{Confusion analysis (\Cref{fig:confusion}).}
For C4, main polyp errors are confusions with landmark (12.4\% of true polyps) and normal mucosa (4.7\%).
\Cref{tab:c4_perclass} reports per-class precision/recall for C4.

\textbf{LOO summary.} C10 mean macro-F1 for C4: 0.910 ($n=1$ fold).
