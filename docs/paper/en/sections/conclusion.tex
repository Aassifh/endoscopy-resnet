\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a convolutional JEPA framework on mask-aware SE-ResNet-50 and a \textbf{reproducible colonoscopy classification benchmark} for screening (3-class) and HyperKvasir-23 fine-grained classification (T1).
On public Simula data, ImageNet transfer remains the strongest baseline for screening; fine-grained tasks show macro-F1 $\approx$0.58 with dual-stream B6 leading supervised runs.
CNN-JEPA fine-tuning is competitive with scratch training on T3 but does not yet validate hypothesis H1.
Future work: polyp histology (ERCPMP, PIBAdb), real unlabeled video pretraining, private multi-center LOO folds, and ViT foundation linear probes.
