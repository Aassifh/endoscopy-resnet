\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a convolutional JEPA framework on mask-aware SE-ResNet-50 for colonoscopy frame classification, with a reproducible three-class benchmark and label-efficiency protocol.
On public Simula data, ImageNet transfer remains the strongest baseline; CNN-JEPA fine-tuning is competitive with scratch training but does not yet validate our primary hypothesis H1.
Future work: real unlabeled video pretraining, private multi-center LOO folds, polyp subtyping with histology-linked datasets, and comparison to ViT foundation linear probes.
